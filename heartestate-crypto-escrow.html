— 01How it works

From wallet to title deed, in four steps.

A clear, compliant path from cryptocurrency to a registered property — every step handled by a licensed party, coordinated by us.

STEP 01

Licensed OTC partner

We introduce you to a licensed OTC desk or regulated local exchange that converts major cryptocurrencies into AED or USD. No grey channels — only regulated counterparties.

STEP 02

Onboarding · KYC / AML

We help you register with the partner and pass KYC and AML — source-of-funds documentation included. This is what makes the money bank- and developer-acceptable.

STEP 03

Conversion to fiat

Your crypto is converted to fiat at an agreed rate. Funds are credited either to your own UAE account or directly to a dedicated escrow account for the purchase.

STEP 04

Escrow & settlement

A dedicated escrow account settles with the developer (off-plan, via the project's regulated trust account) or completes the secondary purchase — released against agreed milestones.

— 03The second route

Don't sell — borrow against it.

How it works

  • BTC, ETH or major stablecoins are pledged as collateral with a specialist lender
  • The loan proceeds buy the residence — settlement is still in dirhams
  • The property is then let, and rental income services the loan
  • Your assets stay yours: you do not crystallise a sale or exit your position
  • Loan-to-value, rate, margin-call terms and custody are all set by the lender

What we flag upfront

  • If the market falls, the lender will call for more collateral — this is the principal risk
  • Terms vary widely: compare the whole agreement, not the headline rate
  • We introduce lenders and run the property side; the loan is taken directly with them
  • Heart of the City does not lend and does not take assets as collateral
Heart of the City holds no VARA, UAE Central Bank, lending or financial-advisory licence. We do not accept, hold, convert, transfer or take custody of virtual assets, and we do not lend. We introduce and coordinate; every regulated step is executed by an independently licensed counterparty that performs its own KYC and AML checks. Nothing here is financial or investment advice.
— 04Questions buyers ask

Frequently asked.

Which cryptocurrencies can be used?

The major liquid assets — typically BTC, ETH and leading stablecoins (USDT, USDC) — are the most straightforward. The exact list and limits are set by the licensed partner at the time of the transaction. Tell us what you hold and we'll confirm.

Is buying property with crypto legal in the UAE?

Crypto itself is not handed to the developer. It is converted to fiat by a licensed partner under full KYC/AML, and the property is then paid for and registered in dirhams like any other transaction. This is what keeps it compliant and acceptable to banks, developers and the Land Department.

Who holds the escrow — is my money safe?

The escrow / trust account is held by a regulated third party, not by us. For off-plan purchases, developer payments go into the project's RERA-regulated escrow account by law. We never custody your funds.

What does it cost and how long does it take?

Conversion fees are set by the OTC/exchange partner and depend on asset and amount; we'll show them transparently before you commit. Onboarding and conversion typically take a few business days once KYC is cleared — timelines depend on the partner and amount.

Does this work for both off-plan and secondary deals?

Yes. For off-plan, funds settle to the developer via the project trust account. For a secondary purchase, the escrow account is used to complete the transfer with the seller. The flow is the same; the destination differs.

Will I get source-of-funds documentation?

Yes — a clean, documented trail is the entire point. You receive the conversion and compliance records you need for the bank, the developer and your own files.
