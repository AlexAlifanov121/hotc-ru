Project Details

Apartment Waterfront Luxury

The Row Saadiyat is an exclusive residential community by Aldar Properties, located in the prestigious Cultural District of Saadiyat Island, Abu Dhabi. Designed by the world-renowned Bjarke Ingels Group (BIG), this architectural masterpiece offers a collection of 1 to 3-bedroom apartments with sizes ranging from 89 to 446 sqm.


The community features seven mid-rise buildings, each nine storeys high, with interiors crafted by Kettle Collective. Residents enjoy generous balconies exceeding 200 sqm, premium finishes and a lifestyle surrounded by world-class museums, galleries and pristine beaches.
