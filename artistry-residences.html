Project Details

Apartment Penthouse Design District

Artistry Residences is a striking twin-tower project by Select Group in Dubai Design District. The architecture features purposeful lines and refined geometry, creating an expressive silhouette. Tower One has 36 levels, Tower Two has 37 levels.


The development features 1-3 bedroom apartments and two-level 4-bedroom penthouses with private pools. Interiors maximize natural light with open layouts and a carefully curated palette of contemporary materials.
