— 01What we do

Clients and families, for the long term.

Four stages — and the deal is only the second of them. Our real work begins after the keys.

How you want to live

A home isn't square metres — it's how your morning goes: the view from the window, the school run, the sea twenty minutes away. That's where we start — then show you the few places where your life will actually settle.

The deal is on us

We take on the whole thing — the figures, the negotiation, the paperwork, the bank. You get ready for the move, instead of drowning in admin and emails.

The first days home

We get the home ready for you: handover from the developer, snagging, water, power and internet connected, help furnishing it. You arrive and simply live in it — no building site, no boxes.

Life here

Then comes Dubai itself. The visa and licence, a school, a doctor, a table for tonight, a yacht for the weekend. We stay close, in your timezone — for as long as Dubai is part of your life.
